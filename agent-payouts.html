Agent Payout Policy

A clear weekly payout workflow.

Approved and payable commissions are processed through the weekly payout cycle, subject to verification and the published payout cut-off.

1. Qualifying transaction

Customer completes an eligible service transaction and required milestones.

2. Verification

Admin verifies service completion, attribution, cancellations and any reversals.

3. Approval

Approved commission appears in the agent ledger with a clear status.

4. Weekly payout

Verified payable balances are included in the weekly payout batch.

Exact weekly cut-off, minimum payout threshold, bank verification, taxes/TDS if applicable, failed transfer handling and reversals must be published in the final policy before payouts begin. No payout timing is guaranteed for disputed or unverified transactions.
