FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Loan Against Property?

Loan Against Property is a property-backed financing option where an eligible property may be offered as security, subject to lender assessment and applicable terms.

Which properties may be considered?

Depending on lender policy, eligible residential, commercial and industrial properties may be considered after legal, technical and valuation assessment.

Can LAP be used for business purposes?

Eligible property-backed financing may support certain business requirements such as expansion or working capital, subject to lender policy and end-use conditions.

What documents are generally required?

KYC documents, income proof, bank statements and property ownership documents may be requested. Exact requirements vary by lender and applicant profile.

Does Fundvexxa guarantee approval?

No. Final eligibility, loan amount, interest rate, tenure, charges and approval are decided by the relevant lender or financial institution.
