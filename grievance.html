Grievance Redressal

We want to understand your concern and help you find the appropriate next step.

How to raise a concern

For a complaint about a Fundvexxa enquiry, communication, service experience or an unresolved support request, contact our team through the official contact channels listed below.

Please include your name, a brief description of the issue and your enquiry reference ID, if available. Do not send OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or banking login credentials.

Open Contact Page →

What happens next?

Our team can review the details you provide and respond with available information or the appropriate next step. Please retain your reference ID and any relevant communication for follow-up.

Fundvexxa is not automatically the lender handling your application. For a complaint about a bank or NBFC's sanction, charges, repayment or loan servicing, you may also need to contact that institution through its official grievance process.
