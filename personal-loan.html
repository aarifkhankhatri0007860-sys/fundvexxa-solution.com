Eligibility

Who may explore personal loan options?

Personal loan eligibility is decided by the relevant lender. The following profiles may explore available options subject to applicable criteria.

Salaried Employees

Employees of private companies, MNCs and eligible government organizations.

Self-Employed Applicants

Individuals with eligible income and business activity as per lender criteria.

Professionals

Eligible professionals such as doctors, consultants, architects and others.

Business Owners

Business owners who meet the applicable income, banking and credit requirements.

Documentation

Common documents

Exact documentation depends on the lender, product and applicant profile. Additional documents may be requested.

Identity & Address

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar or other valid KYC document
  • Address proof where required
  • Recent photograph where applicable

Income & Banking

  • Recent salary slips, where applicable
  • Recent bank statements
  • ITR / income documents where applicable
  • Additional financial documents if requested

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common personal loan questions.

Who can explore personal loan options?

Eligible salaried individuals, self-employed applicants, professionals and business owners may explore available personal loan products, subject to lender criteria.

What documents are commonly required?

Depending on the lender and profile, KYC documents, address proof, income documents and bank statements may be required.

Is personal loan approval guaranteed?

No. Fundvexxa does not guarantee approval. Final approval, amount, rate and terms are decided by the relevant lender.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary depending on the lender, applicant profile, documentation and verification process.
