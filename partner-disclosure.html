Partner & Funding Disclosure

Important information about our role, lender decisions and application process.

1. Our role

Fundvexxa provides funding-related information, enquiry assistance and support with understanding potential loan or business-finance options. The exact service available may depend on the product and the case.

2. Lenders make the final decision

Submitting an enquiry or application does not guarantee eligibility, sanction, a particular interest rate, a loan amount or disbursal. The relevant bank, NBFC or other financing provider independently determines its eligibility criteria, documentation, pricing and final decision.

3. Partner relationships

A bank, NBFC, lender or financial brand should be considered an official Fundvexxa partner only where that relationship has been confirmed and is expressly disclosed. Listing or discussing a financial product does not, by itself, mean that Fundvexxa is authorised by or partnered with its provider. Please contact us to confirm the current status of a specific relationship.

4. Fees and payments

Any applicable service fee, commission arrangement or third-party charge should be explained before you agree to a paid service. Do not pay an individual or account claiming to represent Fundvexxa unless you have independently verified the payment instructions and received clear written details.

5. Personal information and safety

Provide personal or financial documents only through a verified and appropriate channel. Never share an OTP, password, UPI PIN, card PIN or banking login credentials with anyone claiming to process a loan.

Read our Privacy Policy for information about personal data handling.

6. Contact and verification

For questions about an enquiry, service, fee or claimed lender relationship, use our Contact page or request assistance through our official communication channels.

This disclosure describes general service principles. It is not a claim that Fundvexxa is a bank, lender, RBI-regulated entity, or authorised representative of any particular financial institution.
