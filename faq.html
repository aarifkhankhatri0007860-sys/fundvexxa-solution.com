Fundvexxa FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Find clear answers about funding options, eligibility, documentation, government schemes and how the Fundvexxa assistance process works.

What would you like to know?

Here are some common questions customers ask before starting their funding journey.

01

Does Fundvexxa guarantee loan approval?

No. Fundvexxa does not guarantee approval. Loan approval, interest rate, sanctioned amount, tenure and other terms are decided by the relevant lender or financial institution based on its own eligibility and underwriting criteria.

02

What documents may be required?

Requirements vary by product and applicant. Depending on the case, documents may include KYC, bank statements, income proof, ITR, GST records, business proof and other financial or supporting documents.

03

Can a new business apply for funding?

Yes, some loan products and government-supported schemes may be available to new businesses. Other products may require an operating history, turnover or other eligibility criteria. Final eligibility depends on the applicable lender or scheme.

04

How do I start the process?

You can contact our team through the website, call +91 72909 77231 or send your requirement on WhatsApp. Our team can help you understand the relevant funding options and documentation requirements.

05

Does Fundvexxa provide government grants?

Fundvexxa can provide guidance regarding eligible grant, subsidy or institutional funding opportunities where applicable. Availability, eligibility and approval depend on the specific government programme or institution.

06

Can I apply for a business loan without collateral?

Some business funding products may be available without traditional collateral, subject to the lender's policies and the applicant's eligibility. Availability and terms vary by lender and product.

07

Can Fundvexxa help with government schemes?

We can provide guidance on applicable business and government-supported funding programmes. The final eligibility, benefit and approval remain subject to the rules of the relevant scheme and authority.

08

How long does funding take?

Processing time varies depending on the product, documentation, applicant profile and the lender or institution involved. Complete and accurate documents can help avoid unnecessary delays.

09

Can I check my eligibility before applying?

You can share your basic requirement with our team for an initial assessment of potentially suitable funding options. Any preliminary assessment is indicative and does not constitute a loan approval or sanction.

10

Does Fundvexxa charge for every funding enquiry?

Charges, if applicable, depend on the service or assistance involved. Any applicable service fee should be confirmed with Fundvexxa before proceeding.
