Requirements

Eligibility & Common Documents

Requirements vary by product, lender, insurer, government scheme or service provider. The following information is indicative only.

Common Eligibility Considerations

  • Age and applicant profile may be considered.
  • Identity and address information may be required.
  • Income/business profile may be evaluated where applicable.
  • Existing obligations may be considered for financial products.
  • Final eligibility is determined by the relevant provider.

Commonly Requested Documents

  • PAN and identity proof, where applicable.
  • Address proof, where applicable.
  • Income or business documents, where applicable.
  • Banking records, where applicable.
  • Additional documents may be requested during verification.
