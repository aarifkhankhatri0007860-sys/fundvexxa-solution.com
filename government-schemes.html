Explore Funding Routes

Which government-linked route may fit your requirement?

Different programmes serve different purposes. Eligibility, benefits, contribution requirements, documentation and application channels can vary.

Micro Business

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

MUDRA-related credit routes are designed to support eligible micro and small business activities through participating lending institutions.

  • Useful for eligible micro-business requirements
  • Business-purpose credit route
  • Eligibility depends on lender and applicable rules
  • Documentation varies by applicant and lender
New Enterprise

PMEGP

The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme supports eligible new micro-enterprise projects through applicable implementing and lending channels.

  • Relevant for eligible new enterprise projects
  • Project-based application process
  • Project and applicant eligibility apply
  • Sanction and benefit depend on applicable rules
MSME

MSME Funding & Support

MSMEs can explore different credit, guarantee, development and support programmes depending on business activity, stage, sector and eligibility.

  • Business funding and credit support routes
  • May include guarantee-linked mechanisms
  • Business records can be important
  • Programme-specific conditions apply
Startup

Startup India & Startup Funding

Eligible startups can explore government initiatives covering funding, incubation, mentorship, market access and other forms of ecosystem support.

  • Startup-specific support programmes
  • Seed funding opportunities for eligible startups
  • Investor and funding ecosystem support
  • Programme-specific eligibility may apply
Credit Support

Credit Guarantee-Linked Routes

Certain government-supported mechanisms use credit guarantees to facilitate access to eligible business or startup credit. A guarantee does not mean automatic loan approval.

  • Can support eligible credit applications
  • Applicable conditions vary by programme
  • Lender assessment still applies
  • No automatic sanction or approval
Other Programmes

Sector & State-Specific Schemes

Depending on your location, industry and business stage, additional central or state-level programmes may be relevant.

  • State and UT startup policies
  • Sector-specific programmes
  • Incubation and mentoring opportunities
  • Market-access and other support initiatives

Documentation

Keep your basic records ready.

Exact documentation depends on the selected scheme, applicant profile, project and implementing institution.

KYC & Identity

PAN, Aadhaar or other applicable identity and address documentation may be required.

Business Documents

Business registration, Udyam/MSME information, GST details or other applicable business proof may be requested.

Financial Records

Bank statements, ITR, financial statements, turnover details or other records may be required.

Project Details

Project-based programmes may require a business/project report, cost estimates and related information.

Banking Information

Existing banking details, account information and lender records may be relevant during assessment.

Additional Records

The concerned scheme or institution may request additional documents based on the applicant and project.
