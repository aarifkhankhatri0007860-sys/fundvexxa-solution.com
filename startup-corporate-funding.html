Preliminary Assessment

What may influence funding eligibility?

There is no single eligibility formula for every funding product. The relevant lender or provider may consider multiple factors.

Business Factors

  • Business vintage and operating history
  • Business structure and registration
  • Annual turnover and cash-flow profile
  • Industry and business model
  • Existing financial obligations

Funding Factors

  • Purpose of funding
  • Funding requirement and proposed amount
  • Repayment capacity
  • Available financial records
  • Applicable lender/provider criteria
Documentation

Keep your business information organised

Exact documentation depends on the product and provider. Commonly requested information can include the following.

Business Identity

Business registration, constitution and basic business details as applicable.

Financial Information

Relevant financial statements, turnover information, banking information or tax records as applicable.

Business Requirement

Details explaining why funding is required and how the proposed finance is expected to be used.
