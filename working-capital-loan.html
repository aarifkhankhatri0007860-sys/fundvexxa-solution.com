POSSIBLE ROUTES

Working Capital Funding Routes

The appropriate facility depends on business profile, cash-flow pattern, receivables, lender policy and the intended use of funds.

Working Capital Loan

A business finance route that may be considered for eligible short-term working capital requirements.

Cash Credit / Overdraft

Some lenders offer revolving working capital facilities subject to assessment, sanctioned limits and applicable terms.

Receivables / Invoice Finance

Eligible MSMEs may also explore receivables-based routes such as invoice discounting or TReDS where applicable.

Note: TReDS is an electronic platform for discounting MSME receivables from supplies to eligible buyers. It can help address delayed payments and improve liquidity. Availability depends on applicable eligibility and platform/lender requirements.

DOCUMENTATION

Documents Commonly Required

Exact documentation varies by lender, business constitution, facility type and applicant profile.

Business & Financial Documents

  • GST registration and applicable GST returns
  • Udyam / business registration documents where applicable
  • Recent business bank statements
  • ITR returns
  • Profit & Loss statement
  • Balance sheet / financial statements where applicable

KYC & Constitution Documents

  • PAN and identity documents
  • Address proof
  • Proprietorship / partnership documents where applicable
  • Company incorporation and constitutional documents where applicable
  • Business address proof
  • Additional lender-specific documents if required
