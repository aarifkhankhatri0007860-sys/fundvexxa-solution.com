Current Guideline Snapshot

Important PMEGP Project Limits

Ye figures current revised PMEGP guidelines ke margin-money subsidy framework se related hain.

₹50L Manufacturing project cost eligible for margin-money subsidy
₹20L Business / Service project cost eligible for margin-money subsidy
18+ Minimum age for individual applicants for new enterprises
VIII Minimum education condition above specified project thresholds

Important: ₹50 lakh / ₹20 lakh figures margin-money subsidy eligibility ceiling se related hain; ye guaranteed loan amount nahi hain. Final bank finance and project sanction lender appraisal aur applicable guidelines par depend karte hain.

New Project vs Existing Business

  • New PMEGP enterprise assistance eligible new projects ke liye intended hai.
  • Existing PMEGP/REGP/MUDRA units ke upgradation ke liye separate second-loan provisions exist karte hain.
  • Existing business ko simply “PMEGP expansion loan” kehna correct nahi hai; applicable category and conditions verify karni hoti hain.

Important Financial Disclaimer

  • PMEGP approval guaranteed nahi hai.
  • Subsidy guaranteed cash payout nahi hai.
  • Bank finance lender appraisal aur applicable rules ke subject mein hota hai.
  • Project cost, contribution, subsidy, interest, repayment and other terms current guidelines and sanction ke according final hote hain.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

PMEGP assistance se related common questions.

What is PMEGP?
PMEGP, Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme, Government of India ka entrepreneurship and employment generation programme hai jo eligible micro enterprise projects ko support framework provide karta hai.
Can a new entrepreneur apply?
Haan, applicable guidelines ke according 18 years se above eligible individual new enterprise ke liye PMEGP explore kar sakta hai.
What is the maximum PMEGP project cost?
Current revised guidelines ke according margin-money subsidy ke liye maximum project cost manufacturing sector mein ₹50 lakh aur business/service sector mein ₹20 lakh hai. Iska matlab guaranteed loan amount nahi hai.
Is PMEGP subsidy guaranteed?
Nahi. Applicant eligibility, project viability, documentation, implementing agency process, bank appraisal aur applicable PMEGP rules ke according final decision hota hai.
Can an existing business apply for PMEGP?
New enterprise assistance new projects ke liye intended hai. Eligible existing PMEGP/REGP/MUDRA units ke upgradation ke liye separate second-loan provisions ho sakte hain, subject to specific conditions.
Does Fundvexxa guarantee approval?
Nahi. Fundvexxa documentation, project preparation aur application guidance provide karta hai. Final sanction, finance aur subsidy-related decision relevant authorities aur lender ke discretion aur applicable rules par depend karta hai.
