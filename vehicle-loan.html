Eligibility & Documents

Who May Enquire?

Final eligibility is determined by the relevant lender based on income, credit profile, vehicle details, documentation and other applicable criteria.

Common Applicant Profiles

  • Salaried employees with qualifying income profiles
  • Self-employed individuals with verifiable income
  • Business owners with relevant financial records
  • Professionals with qualifying income profiles
  • Applicants purchasing eligible personal vehicles
  • Eligible businesses requiring commercial vehicles

Documents That May Be Required

  • Identity and address proof
  • PAN and other documents where required by the lender
  • Salary slips or income documents
  • Bank statements
  • ITR / business financial documents where applicable
  • Vehicle quotation and vehicle-related details
