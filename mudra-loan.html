Understanding PMMY

What Is Mudra Loan?

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) was launched to improve access to institutional credit for micro enterprises. The scheme can support eligible income-generating activities, including manufacturing, trading, services and certain activities allied to agriculture.

01

Business Expansion

Existing eligible businesses may explore funding for expansion, assets, stock and other permitted business requirements.

02

New Business

Eligible entrepreneurs starting a micro business can explore applicable PMMY options based on their profile and lender requirements.

03

Working Capital

PMMY can cover eligible term-loan and working-capital requirements through participating lending institutions.

Current PMMY Categories

Which Mudra Category May Apply?

Current official PMMY categories are based on the loan amount. Tarun Plus has an additional previous-loan repayment condition.

Category 01

Shishu

Up to ₹50,000

Designed for smaller funding requirements, subject to lender assessment and applicable conditions.

Category 02

Kishor

Above ₹50,000 to ₹5 Lakh

Suitable for eligible businesses requiring a higher level of business finance.

Category 03

Tarun

Above ₹5 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh

For eligible business requirements within the Tarun category limit.

Category 04

Tarun Plus

Above ₹10 Lakh to ₹20 Lakh

Applicable to entrepreneurs who have availed and successfully repaid a previous Tarun-category loan, subject to conditions.

*Loan amount, eligibility, pricing, repayment terms and approval are subject to the applicable PMMY guidelines and the decision of the relevant lending institution.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions before exploring PMMY.

What is Mudra Loan?

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is a Government of India initiative designed to improve institutional credit access for eligible micro enterprises.

What are the current Mudra Loan categories?

Shishu is up to ₹50,000; Kishor is above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh; Tarun is above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh; and Tarun Plus is above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh, subject to the applicable condition for previous successful Tarun repayment.

Is collateral required?

The Department of Financial Services describes PMMY as providing collateral-free credit. Individual lender requirements and applicable scheme conditions should still be checked.

Can a new business apply?

Eligible new entrepreneurs can explore applicable PMMY options. Final eligibility and approval depend on the relevant lender's assessment.

Does Fundvexxa guarantee approval?

No. Fundvexxa provides guidance and application assistance. Final approval, amount, pricing, tenure and other terms are decided by the relevant lending institution.

Where can I learn more about PMMY?

You can check the official Department of Financial Services PMMY page and the official MUDRA website for the latest scheme information.
