Partner FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can become a Fundvexxa Partner?

Individuals, sales professionals, business owners and other eligible persons interested in financial product distribution or customer assistance can apply. Final approval is subject to program requirements.

Do I get a personal dashboard?

Approved partners can receive access to a personal dashboard where available features may include leads, products, application status, training resources and partner information.

Can I build my own team?

Eligible partners may participate in referral or team-building programs where such features are enabled and subject to applicable program terms.

Will registration guarantee income?

No. Registration or membership does not guarantee leads, approvals, commissions or income. Earnings depend on actual business activity, product eligibility and applicable program terms.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your application is stored for review. The Fundvexxa team can verify your details and membership selection before activating partner access.
