Who we are

A business support partner from the first conversation.

Every business requirement is different. The right funding route can depend on the business profile, purpose, financial position, documents and the criteria of the relevant institution.

Fundvexxa's approach is to first understand the requirement, then help customers explore potentially relevant funding categories and prepare for the next step.

Alongside funding support, we also provide business-focused digital services including websites, marketing and app development support.
