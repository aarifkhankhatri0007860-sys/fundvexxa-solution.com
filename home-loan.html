Why Home Financing

Plan a large property investment with structured repayment.

A home loan can spread an eligible property cost across a repayment schedule rather than requiring the full purchase or construction cost upfront. The actual loan amount, margin, tenure, rate and charges depend on the lender and borrower profile.

Eligibility

Who may explore a home loan?

Eligibility is assessed by the lender. Common assessment areas include income, repayment capacity, employment or business profile, credit history, property details and supporting documents.

A

Salaried Individuals

Employees in eligible private, public-sector or government employment may apply subject to lender policy and profile assessment.

B

Self-Employed Professionals

Professionals such as doctors, consultants, architects and other eligible professionals may be assessed using income and business records.

C

Business Owners

Proprietors, partners and company directors may explore options based on business performance, income and financial documentation.

Documentation

Documents commonly requested

The exact checklist varies by lender, applicant profile, property type and transaction. Keep the following categories ready for initial discussion.

Identity & Address

PAN, Aadhaar or other accepted KYC documents, address proof and other lender-requested identification.

Income & Banking

Salary slips, bank statements, ITR/Form 16 and business financial records where applicable.

Property Documents

Sale agreement, title/ownership records, sanctioned plans, tax records and other documents requested for legal and technical verification.

Property Types

Residential properties that may be considered

Property eligibility is always subject to lender policy, legal/technical verification and the location and status of the property.

Apartments & Flats

Ready-to-move or under-construction residential units, subject to project and lender requirements.

Independent Houses

Eligible independent residential homes subject to title, valuation and other checks.

Villa Properties

Residential villas and eligible projects subject to lender and property assessment.

Self-Construction

Construction of a residential property on eligible land with required approvals and documents.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can self-employed professionals apply for a home loan?

Eligible self-employed professionals and business owners may apply, subject to lender assessment, income records, property details and documentation.

Can a home loan be used for construction?

Home financing may be available for eligible residential construction projects, subject to lender and property requirements.

What documents are commonly required?

Identity and address proof, income documents, bank statements and property-related documents are commonly requested. The exact checklist varies by lender and applicant profile.

Can financing be available for renovation?

Depending on the applicant profile, property and lender policy, financing options may be available for eligible renovation or home improvement requirements.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. The form only creates an enquiry. Approval, rate, amount, tenure and other terms are determined by the relevant lender after its assessment.
